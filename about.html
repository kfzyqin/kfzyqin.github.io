Pleased to Meet You

Dr. Zhenyue Qin is a Researcher at NEC Laboratories America San Jose, fortunately working with Prof. Manmohan Chandraker, where his work focuses on 3D computer vision, vision-language-action models, and autonomous driving. Previously, he was a Postdoctoral Associate at Yale University, a Senior Researcher at Tencent, and a Machine Intelligence Scientist at Seeing Machines. He completed both his PhD and undergraduate studies at the Australian National University, where he was awarded a full-tuition scholarship for his doctoral studies and received a government prize as an undergraduate, graduating with First-Class Honors.
