Pleased to meet you.

Dr Zhenyue Qin is currently a Postdoctoral Associate at Yale University working on vision large language models. He also serves as an Associate PhD Supervisor at the Australian National University (ANU). Previously, he was a Senior Researcher at Tencent. Dr Qin is an active member of the AI research community, serving as a Reviewer, Area Chair, and Associate Editor for prestigious conferences and journals. He completed both his PhD and undergraduate studies at ANU, where he was awarded a full-tuition scholarship for his doctoral studies and received a government prize as an undergraduate, graduating with First-Class Honors. His research focuses on vision large language models, healthcare AI, and 3D computer vision.
