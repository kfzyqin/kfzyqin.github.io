Pleased to Meet You

Dr. Zhenyue Qin is a researcher in 3D computer vision. He completed both his undergraduate and PhD studies at the Australian National University, where he received a government prize as an undergraduate, graduated with First-Class Honors, and was awarded a full-tuition scholarship for his doctoral studies, during which he was fortunate to study under Prof. Bob McKay, Prof. Tom Gedeon, and Prof. Dongwoo Kim. He later continued his training as a Postdoctoral Associate at Yale University, where he had the privilege of working with Prof. Qingyu Chen and Prof. Hua Xu. In industry, he worked as a Senior Researcher at Tencent XR Vision Labs, where he was grateful to learn from Prof. Hongdong Li, and as a Machine Intelligence Scientist at Seeing Machines, working directly with Principal Scientist Akshay Asthana. He is now a Researcher at NEC Laboratories America in San Jose, and is honored to work with Prof. Manmohan Chandraker. His current research is on Physical AI, built on vision-language-action models, with applications including autonomous driving and robotics.
